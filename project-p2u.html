Pharmacy 2 U: AI-powered smart reminders

AI powered smart reminders, a predictive model identifying when patients are due medication and proactively triggering dispensing.

UX UI Research Healthcare

The challenge

Patients miss doses, forget to reorder, or just disengage from managing repeat prescriptions. The problem wasn't awareness, it was friction. The challenge was designing an intervention that felt timely enough to act on, not just another notification to dismiss.

Concept and research

I worked on this from concept, starting with research into how patients actually manage prescriptions day-to-day. We spoke to patients across different medication types and mapped the end-to-end journey to understand where people were dropping off and what a proactive nudge needed to feel like to be trusted.

Design and testing

I designed the patient-facing experience around the predictive model: the reminder surface, confirmation flow, and edge case states. I ran multiple rounds of user testing, refining the interaction and copy each time. Small details mattered a lot here. We tested different tones and entry points until we landed on something that felt trustworthy and easy to act on.
